## 1. Where this stands, in one page

**The venture is not a bakery.** It is a **shelf-stable ration and civil-preparedness pack assembler**: you buy components from existing Slovak producers, assemble and certify complete packs, and sell them to the state, to municipalities and eventually to NATO and export buyers. Later, you get paid to *hold and rotate* those packs as reserve stock — which is the part that becomes an annuity.

**Why this and not the €600k bakery.** Two advisory documents you commissioned contradicted each other: the dossier's own scoring sheet ranked the bakery #1 of 6, while Addendum A rejected it. I resolved it with arithmetic rather than opinion — the scoring sheet had **no criterion for capital at risk before revenue**. Add that single column at a 20% weight and the ranking inverts completely: broker 3.92, kitting 3.80, reserve service 3.68, **bakery 3.28 — last**. Addendum A was right, for a reason it never actually stated.

**The ladder you are climbing.** Tier 0 broker → Tier 1 kitter → Tier 2 reserve holder → Tier 3 own production. The factory is fully specified and sits behind **Gate 1 (31/12/2027)**: two co-packing agreements, €300k of contracted demand, and a grant decision in hand. Not a euro of plant capex before that.

**Your own sequence, which is correct:** samples → conversations → contracts → assembly. No phase funds the next with your money.

**The proof this market is real.** The Slovak MoD tendered **€711,375** of combat rations in May 2026 and received **zero bids**. It re-tendered in July. **(F)** Demand grew 6.9× in a year and the market did not answer. That is not an opportunity you are asserting — it is a procurement record, and it goes in every application and every first meeting.

**What you missed, and why it barely matters.** The regionálny príspevok closed 05/10 without a filing. It was a 45%-rated share of €13.9m across 30 districts. The replacement is better: the **JTF productive-investments call — €16.89m across seven districts, Rimavská Sobota eligible, start-ups explicitly in scope, opening Q4 2026–Q1 2027.** Removing the lapsed leg moved the portfolio's probability of at least one success from 99.99% to **99.98%**.

## 3. Five calls. Here are the questions.

**(O) This is the trip. Everything else is scenery.** Five calls, all free, and between them they close most of the open questions in the whole plan. Slovak phrasing given because you will be making these in Slovak; all in vykanie.

### Call 1 — ÚPSVaR Rimavská Sobota · THE MOST VALUABLE CALL
*Opening:* „Dobrý deň, pripravujem založenie výrobnej firmy v okrese Rimavská Sobota a potreboval by som informácie k príspevkom na zamestnávanie."

1. **„Príspevok podľa §50 pre uchádzača vedeného v evidencii aspoň 24 mesiacov — je suma 1 291,17 € mesačná alebo jednorazová? Ak mesačná, na koľko mesiacov sa poskytuje v našom okrese?"**
2. „Koľko uchádzačov spĺňajúcich podmienky absolventskej praxe podľa §51 je momentálne vedených v evidencii v okrese?"
3. „Ktoré nástroje sú aktuálne otvorené — §50, §54, §53d — a aký je požadovaný postup a poradie krokov?"
4. „Môže uchádzač o zamestnanie pracovať na dohodu a zostať v evidencii, ak jeho zárobok nepresiahne životné minimum?"
5. „Môžem tieto informácie dostať písomne alebo e-mailom?"

**Why it matters:** question 1 changes your hiring plan by an order of magnitude. Question 2 tells you whether §51 graduate practice — 20 h/week, ÚPSVaR pays them, you pay nothing — has any candidates locally, or whether it is a paper option. **(R) Expect few: the RS pool is long-term unemployed adults, not recent graduates.**

**Trap to avoid:** these subsidies are for *newly created* jobs and generally need the agreement signed **before** employment starts. Hire first and you get nothing.

## 4. If you view premises

Walk the place with this list. **(R) Extraction is the item most often stripped when a kitchen closes**, and it is expensive to replace.

**Check:** flooring coved, impervious, sound · walls washable to height · **mechanical extraction present and working** · hot water at every sink · hand-wash stations (elbow/foot taps) · floor drainage and **grease trap** · separate clean/unclean routes · changing area and WC · lockable, vermin-proof dry storage · **three-phase power, and the reserved capacity in kW** · fenced and alarmed.

**Ask the landlord:** the previous RVPS registration number · **why it closed** · what was removed on closure · reserved electrical capacity in writing · **12-month term with a break clause and no fit-out obligation**.

**Benchmark, so you are not overcharged (F):** halls in Rimavská Sobota €2.50/m²/month excl. utilities, with ~450 m² and ~300 m² units available · 122 m² high-storage at €250/month · a 200 m² unit near the bus and train station · Tornaľa 3,000–10,500 m² at €3.25/m²/month (too big, but a price anchor).

**(O) Do not buy.** A 427 m² hall in the district was listed at **€178,000 (F)** — roughly your entire Tier 1 capex, for a roof that earns nothing.

## 5. If you meet a co-packer

Five questions. The third is the one that decides whether you have a business.

1. „Aké je vaše minimálne objednávacie množstvo na položku a na výrobnú dávku?"
2. „Aká je lehota od objednávky po pripravenosť tovaru? Je možné rezervovať kapacitu rámcovou zmluvou?"
3. **„Akú trvanlivosť viete garantovať a za akých skladovacích podmienok? Je podložená skúškami v reálnom čase alebo zrýchlenými?"**
4. „Aké systémy kvality máte zavedené a aký je rozsah a platnosť certifikátu?"
5. „Vyrábate pod značkou odberateľa?"

**Why question 3 is the critical path — and this is the most important thing in the dossier.** Buyers do not buy a recipe. They buy a **guaranteed remaining shelf life**, 24–36 months, written into the contract and audited. **(F)** The evidence standard expects real-time data to confirm accelerated testing — read naively, that is a two-year blocker before you can sell anything.

**It dissolves because you are not the manufacturer.** Your co-packers already hold validated shelf-life data; they already sell those products with best-before dates. So:
- **The pack's shelf life is the shortest component's remaining life** — arithmetic from supplier declarations, not a study you run.
- Your only validation is whether **your assembly and outer pack** compromise it. Small and cheap.
- **Component choice becomes the lever.** Crispbread at ≤10% moisture **(F)** with 24 months, plus a retort meal at 36, gives you a 24-month pack with no long-term trial.

**(O) So collect their written shelf-life declarations before you put a figure in front of any buyer.** Never write a shelf life on a sample label you cannot evidence — a defence buyer will ask for the data, and an unsupported number is worse than none.

**Question 4 also pays for the trip:** if your co-packers hold IFS or BRCGS for the manufactured components, you may need **only HACCP** for the assembly step. That is the largest certification saving the whole model allows.

## 7. The traps

| Trap | Why |
|---|---|
| **Hiring to capture a subsidy** | At 20–50 sample packs you need zero staff. The subsidy never covers the management time |
| **Buying "packaging certification"** | BRCGS Packaging Materials and IFS PACsecure certify *making* packaging. Putting food *into* packaging is a food-safety scope (IFS Food PS 10). Valid certificate, useless commercially |
| **Certifying to FSSC 22000 v6** | **(F)** v7 launched May 2026. Certifying to v6 now forces a second audit within ~18 months |
| **Believing the 60% grant figure** | It is a state-aid *ceiling*, not an offer. National investment aid maxima are 25%/35%. At 35% the old €600k plan had a **€90,000 hole** |
| **Trusting a checkweigher to catch kitting errors** | I modelled it: **only 9 of 20 components are detectable**. A missing coffee sachet is 0.4σ. And errors cancel — +1 jam / −1 honey nets 5 g, invisible. Use a **kit nest tray** (€300–1,500); it works on a 1 g sachet and a 300 g pouch alike |
| **Buying computer vision** | €15–40k industrial, and a defence buyer will not accept a homemade classifier unvalidated. But **do** buy a €200–600 camera to photograph every open tray as *evidence* — best €400 in the budget |
| **Forgetting working capital** | The state pays ~60 days after invoice while you pay suppliers up front. **This is what kills lean food startups** |
| **General instead of product liability insurance** | General does not cover a recall, and you are putting food in people's mouths |
| **Leading with "war"** | Lead with food security and civil preparedness. Same market, how the EU frames it, and it keeps grant assessors and municipal officials comfortable. Cite notice numbers, not adjectives |
| **Discussing the site publicly** | **(F)** There was a thwarted arson plot against a Slovak drone facility in 08/2026. Once defence-adjacent, discretion is a control, not theatre |
