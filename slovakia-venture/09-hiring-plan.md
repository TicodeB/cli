# 09 — Hiring Plan (gated)

**Conclusion first (O): you hire nobody in 2026, and nobody in 2027 until Gate 1.** Tier 0 is you plus an accountant. The "hire cycle for the factory" in the original brief is a Tier 1/Tier 3 activity, and running it early is precisely how the Downside case in the financial model happens — the premortem names "the plant ran at 35% utilisation, where fixed costs eat everything."

What follows is the hiring plan you execute **when the gate opens**, plus the subsidy mechanics that make Rimavská Sobota unusually favourable for it.

---

## 1. The labour market — genuinely your strongest card

**(F)** Rimavská Sobota has the **highest unemployment in Slovakia** and is the **only district above 10%**:
- **Podiel disponibilných uchádzačov (PDU): 10.44%** (June 2026) — the highest in Slovakia
- **10.41%** (July 2026), again the highest and the only district over 10%
- The local labour office simultaneously registers **136 unfilled vacancies**
- **(F)** RS has been the district with Slovakia's highest unemployment for many years

**(R) Data-quality flag — do not quote these interchangeably.** One source reports RS at **14.3%** (April 2026) while others report ~10.4% (June/July 2026). These are almost certainly **different metrics** — *miera evidovanej nezamestnanosti* vs. *podiel disponibilných uchádzačov o zamestnanie* (PDU) — not a collapse in unemployment over two months. **Before quoting any figure in a grant application, take it from the ÚPSVaR monthly statistics and name the metric.** A grant assessor will know the difference, and getting it wrong undermines everything else in the document.

**(O) What this means operationally.** Labour availability is not your constraint — labour *quality and supervision* will be. A district with 10%+ availability and 136 unfilled vacancies tells you there is a **skills and expectations mismatch**, not an absence of people. Budget for training and for one genuinely good supervisor, and do not assume a large candidate pool means an easy hire.

## 3. ÚPSVaR subsidies — real money, and it scales with local unemployment

**(F)** The instruments that apply:

### § 50 — Príspevok na podporu zamestnávania znevýhodneného UoZ
- Paid to an employer who takes on a **disadvantaged jobseeker** registered for **at least 3 months**.
- **(F) In 2026 the contribution is €1,291.17** for jobseekers registered at least **24 months**.
- **(F) Critically: the amount and duration depend on the average registered unemployment rate in the district** where the job is created, and on the employer's legal form and activity.
- **(O) Rimavská Sobota has the highest rate in Slovakia. This instrument pays more here than anywhere else in the country.** That is a real, quantifiable advantage of the location and it belongs in the grant narrative.

### § 54 — Príspevok na podporu vytvorenia pracovného miesta
- **(F)** An active labour-market measure under the employment services act; the created job must last **at least 3 months**.
- **(O)** Delivered through national projects whose terms change; check what is currently open rather than relying on the statute.

### § 51 / § 53d and related
- **(F)** § 53d is a contribution for newly created jobs; § 51 covers graduate practice **(R)**.
- **(O)** Graduate practice is worth a look at Tier 1: a low-cost way to trial candidates for the supervisor role.

**(F) Procedure:** binding information must be obtained from the **ÚPSVaR office in the territorial district where the job will be created** — i.e. the Rimavská Sobota office, not a national helpline.

**Sources:** [ÚPSVaR §50](https://www.upsvr.gov.sk/sluzby-zamestnanosti/nastroje-aktivnych-opatreni-trhu-prace/prispevky-pre-zamestnavatela/prispevok-na-podporu-zamestnavania-znevyhodneneho-uchadzaca-o-zamestnanie-50.html?page_id=293050) · [§54 leaflet](https://www.upsvr.gov.sk/buxus/docs/urady/LM/ESF/%C2%A7_54_Sanca_na_zamestnanie_-_Letak.pdf) · [IZ — §50](https://www.iz.sk/sk/projekty/zakon-o-sluzbach-zamestnanosti/paragraf-50) · [IZ — §53d](https://www.iz.sk/sk/projekty/zakon-o-sluzbach-zamestnanosti/paragraf-53d)

> **(O) Sequencing trap that costs the whole subsidy.** These contributions are for **newly created** jobs and generally require the agreement to be in place **before** the employment starts. Hire first and apply later and you get nothing. The ÚPSVaR conversation therefore belongs **before** the first job advert — put it in the Gate 1 checklist, not the onboarding checklist.
>
> **(O) Second trap: double funding.** If a grant (`05`) funds job creation for the same post, you cannot also claim the ÚPSVaR contribution for the same cost. Map each post to one instrument.

## 4. The hire cycle — the actual process, for when the gate opens

**(O)** Built for 5 hires in a district with high availability and a skills mismatch. Six weeks from decision to start.

| Week | Step | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| **−2** | **ÚPSVaR meeting first** | Confirm which posts qualify under §50/§54/§53d, the amounts, and the agreement sequence. **Nothing else starts until this is done** |
| 1 | Define roles | One page per role: tasks, physical requirements, food-hygiene basics, shift pattern. Keep it concrete |
| 1 | Post | ÚPSVaR vacancy register (free, and it is where the disadvantaged-jobseeker pool is) · profesia.sk · local: rimava.sk, district FB groups |
| 2 | Screen | Phone screen for reliability signals: commute, shift availability, work history continuity |
| 3 | Assess | **A practical test, not an interview.** 45 minutes of actual kitting against a checklist and a target weight. This predicts performance in this role; a conversation does not |
| 4 | Offer + medicals | Occupational-health assessment for food handling; food-hygiene training booked |
| 5 | Contracts | Slovak employment contract, probation (max 3 months), H&S and HACCP induction documented |
| 6 | Start, staggered | Supervisor starts **2 weeks before** operators, to build the SOPs they will be trained on |

**(O) The two rules that matter most:**
1. **Supervisor first, always.** One competent supervisor who owns the SOPs and the quality records is worth more than three extra operators. In a district with a skills mismatch this is the hire to overpay for.
2. **Practical test over interview.** For kitting accuracy, a timed test against a checkweigher target is a direct measure of the job. Interviews measure interviewing.
