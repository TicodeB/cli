## 3. What this does to the incorporation decision

**(O) Incorporation is no longer optional-but-sensible. It is the prerequisite for the lead instrument, and the timeline now works.**

```
NOW (06/10)          incorporate: 7-10 working days  →  IČO by ~20/10
                                                         │
Q4 2026 / Q1 2027    JTF call published ────────────────┤
                                                         │
                     application prepared and submitted  │
                                                         ▼
mid-2027 (R)         decision  ──────────────────────────┐
                                                         │
31/12/2027           GATE 1: >=2 co-packing agreements   │
                     + >=EUR300k demand                  │
                     + GRANT DECISION IN HAND  ◄─────────┘
```

**(O) Note how cleanly this resolves the tension I expected to have to argue about.** Addendum A's Gate 1 forbids capex before contracted demand; a capex grant seems to pull the other way. It does not, for two reasons:
1. **Grant rules forbid starting before approval anyway** (`05` §7) — applying creates no commitment.
2. **"Grant decision in hand" is already one of Gate 1's three conditions.** Applying in Q4 2026 and deciding in mid-2027 puts the decision exactly where Gate 1 wants it.

So the JTF application is not a deviation from the gated plan. **It is the mechanism by which Gate 1's third condition gets satisfied.**

## 4. The narrative — and why it is unusually strong here

**(O) Grant assessors fund projects that match the programme's own language. JTF's language is a gift to this venture.**

| JTF criterion **(F)** | Your evidence |
|---|---|
| "Economic diversification, modernisation and reconversion" | A district with Slovakia's highest unemployment gaining a new manufacturing capability in a sector it has none of |
| **"New, emerging and transformed sectors"** | European civil preparedness and defence-adjacent food security is demonstrably new: **EU Stockpiling Strategy COM(2025) 528**, **Preparedness Union Strategy (03/2025)** |
| Job creation in a transitioning district | 5 FTE at Tier 1, 13–17 at Tier 3 (`09`) |
| Productive investment in long-term assets | The Tier 1 kitting line (`08`) |
| SME / start-up | A new s.r.o. seated in Rimavská Sobota |

**(O) And the strongest single exhibit is one almost no applicant can produce: documented market failure.** The Slovak MoD tendered €711,375 of combat rations and received **zero bids — twice (F)**. That is not a market opportunity you are asserting; it is a procurement record. Put the TED notice numbers in the application.

**(R) One honest caution on the narrative:** avoid framing this as a *defence* project. JTF is a cohesion instrument, not a defence one, and some assessors will be uncomfortable. Frame it as **food security and civil preparedness** — which is accurate, dual-use, and what the EU's own strategies call it. `Addendum A` makes the same point in its risk table.
