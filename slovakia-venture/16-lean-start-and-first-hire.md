**(O) And the first pushback: do not hire anyone yet.** `09` §2 says no hires before Gate 1, and at 20–50 sample packs you need zero staff. ## 5. "Can unemployed people get off the register?" — the answer is better than you expect

**(O) You asked it as a binary. It is not — and the most useful option keeps them ON the register.**

**(F) From 01/07/2026 a registered jobseeker may earn up to €295.22/month — the living minimum — and remain in the jobseeker register.**

That single fact creates a genuinely humane and cheap starting arrangement: you can engage someone for roughly **56 hours a month at the minimum hourly rate** and they keep their registered status, their benefits position and their safety net. If the venture stalls at Gate 0, they have lost nothing. **(O) For a district with Slovakia's highest unemployment, that asymmetry matters — you are not asking someone to gamble their status on your start-up.**

| Route | Register status | Your monthly cost | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **§51 absolventská praxa** | **Stays on register** | **€0** | Education from day 1 — see §6 |
| **Dohoda (DPČ) at the earnings cap** | **Stays on register** | **€402.09** (€295.22 gross + 36.2%) | A few hours a week, flexible, low risk to them |
| **Full employment at minimum wage** | **Comes off register** | **€1,246.23** | Real role, post-Gate 1 |
| **Full employment + §50 subsidy** | **Comes off register** | **€1,246.23 − €1,291.17 subsidy** | The RS labour pool. See §7 |

## 6. Education from day 1 — §51 is exactly this instrument

**(F) §51 absolventská praxa (graduate practice):**
- Graduate under **26**, completed full-time education **less than two years ago**, with **no regular paid employment since**
- Registered as a jobseeker for **at least one month**
- **20 hours per week**, for **3–6 months**, no extension and no repeat
- **The graduate stays in the jobseeker register throughout**
- **ÚPSVaR pays the graduate** a monthly allowance equal to the **living minimum (€295.22)**, plus reimbursement of accident insurance premium up to **€15**
- **The employer does not pay the allowance**

**(O) So the honest answer to "education from day 1 at what cost": €0 in wages, and your time.** You get 20 h/week for up to six months; they get paid by the state, keep their register status, and leave with real experience. It is close to an ideal fit for a sample-and-trial phase.

**⚠️ But the honest caveat, and it is a real one.** §51 requires **under 26 and a recent full-time graduate**. **(F)** The Rimavská Sobota pool is dominated by **long-term unemployed adults**, not recent graduates — that is what a 10.4% PDU in a structurally weak district means (`09` §1). **(R) Do not assume a §51 candidate exists locally.** Ask ÚPSVaR RS how many registered §51-eligible graduates there actually are in the district before planning around it. If the answer is "few", §50 (§7) is your instrument.

**Sources:** [ÚPSVaR §51](https://www.upsvr.gov.sk/sluzby-zamestnanosti/nastroje-aktivnych-opatreni-trhu-prace/prispevky-pre-obcana/51-prispevok-na-vykonavanie-absolventskej-praxe.html?page_id=12940) · [IZ — §51](https://www.iz.sk/sk/projekty/zakon-o-sluzbach-zamestnanosti/paragraf-51)

## 7. Employment subsidies — and a number you must verify

**(F) §50 — príspevok na podporu zamestnávania znevýhodneného uchádzača o zamestnanie:** for an employer taking on a disadvantaged jobseeker registered at least 3 months. **In 2026 the contribution is €1,291.17 for a jobseeker registered at least 24 months.** The amount and duration **depend on the average registered unemployment rate in the district** where the job is created.

> ⚠️ **(O) Look at that against §4 and then go and verify it.** Total cost of labour at minimum wage is **€1,246.23/month**. The §50 contribution is **€1,291.17**. If that is a **monthly** payment, a minimum-wage hire in Rimavská Sobota is **cost-neutral or better** while it runs. If it is a **one-off**, it covers roughly one month.
>
> **I do not know which, and it is the single highest-value question you can ask ÚPSVaR.** The answer changes your hiring plan by an order of magnitude. Do not build a plan on it until you have it in writing.

**(O) And RS pays the most.** Because the amount scales with district unemployment, and RS has Slovakia's highest, this instrument is worth more here than anywhere else in the country. That is a genuine, quantifiable advantage of the location — put it in the JTF application narrative (`12` §4), where job creation is a scored criterion.

**Other instruments (F):** **§54** — contribution for creating a job for disadvantaged jobseekers, job must last at least 3 months, delivered through national projects whose terms change. **§53d** — contribution for newly created jobs.

### Two sequencing traps
1. **(O) These are for *newly created* jobs and generally require the agreement in place *before* employment starts.** Hire first, apply later, get nothing. **The ÚPSVaR conversation belongs before the first job advert.**
2. **(O) No double funding.** If the JTF grant funds job creation for the same post, you cannot also claim §50 for the same cost. Map each post to exactly one instrument.
