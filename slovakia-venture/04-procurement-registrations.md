# 04 — Procurement & NATO Registrations Runbook

**Principle (O):** registrations are the cheapest asset in this venture. Every one below is free or near-free, and each is a precondition for revenue you cannot access without it. None of them require a factory, premises, certification or staff. Do all the free ones before spending a euro on anything physical.

**Ordering principle (O):** national before allied. Slovak MoD call-offs are the size a two-person company can win (**F:** individual awards of €4,680 and €6,900 found in the MO SR food DNS — Addendum A, TED 217028-2026 / 230329-2026). NSPA contracts are not. Build the reference in Slovakia, then use it at NATO.

## 2. Slovak national registrations

### 2.1 ÚVO — Zoznam hospodárskych subjektov (ZHS)
**What it is (F):** the Office for Public Procurement's register of economic operators. Once listed, the data in it no longer has to be re-proven in each individual procurement.
**Why it matters (F):** registration also satisfies the condition for trading on the Electronic Marketplace (EKS), and during its validity you do not need to re-prove capacity to contract in public procurement.
**Validity (F):** three years.
**Where:** [ÚVO — Registre o hospodárskych subjektoch](https://www.uvo.gov.sk/zaujemca-uchadzac/registre-o-hospodarskych-subjektoch) · forms at [Formuláre a žiadosti](https://www.uvo.gov.sk/zaujemca-uchadzac/registre-o-hospodarskych-subjektoch/formulare-a-ziadosti)
**Cost (R):** administrative fee only, low tens of euros. **Effort:** one application, plus the §32 personal-status evidence.
**Verify:** current fee and exact document list on the ÚVO forms page before submitting.

> **(O) This is the single highest-leverage piece of paperwork in the venture.** It converts every future tender from "assemble 6 certificates" into "tick a box." Do it first.

### 2.2 DNS entries — the permanently open doors
**What a DNS is (F):** an electronic procedure for awarding above-threshold contracts for goods commonly available on the market, set up for a defined period of **max 4 years**, conducted entirely electronically. Critically, **the system stays open for its whole life** — suppliers can apply at any time, not only at launch, and newly-formed entities can qualify mid-life.
**Why this is the entry (O):** it is the only route into state supply that requires no plant, no grant and no investor. Addendum A rates this Improvement #1 and it is correct.

Targets, per Addendum A's TED research **(F, to re-verify — see §5)**:

| Buyer | System | Stated value | Notice |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ministry of Defence SR | Food & drink for soldiers/staff | €13,166,370 | TED 351086-2026 |
| Government Office (ÚV SR) | Food | €3.2m | TED 230756-2026 |
| Žilina self-governing region | Food | €14.27m | TED 234868-2026 |
| Ministry of Interior (MV SR) | Protective equipment & supplies | — | per Addendum A |
| Banská Bystrica region + RS district institutions | Food (schools, social care, hospitals) | — | **to be searched** |

**Platform note (F):** MO SR procures via **eZakazky** ([ezakazky.sk/mosr](https://www.ezakazky.sk/mosr/index.cfm)), which handles contract announcement, registration of interested parties, and electronic communication. Other buyers use **JOSEPHINE**, **EVO** (ÚVO's own), **ERANET** or **EKS**. **(O) You will need accounts on all of them.** They are free. Create them in one sitting.

**What a DNS application for participation contains (F):** proof that you meet the participation conditions, **plus** information on the range of goods/services you regularly offer relevant to the anticipated purchases. That second part is a positioning document, not a form — write it as a capability statement.

**(R) Realistic expectation:** Tier 0 broker margins of 6–12% on €150–300k of call-offs in year one ≈ €12–30k gross. Not a living. It buys the three things that gate everything else: a state-buyer reference, six months of trading history (an SZRB loan condition), and real cost data.

### 2.3 MO SR — Kvalifikačný systém dodávateľov
**What it is (F):** the Ministry of Defence's own supplier qualification system, published at [mosr.sk/kvalifikacny-system-dodavatelov](https://www.mosr.sk/kvalifikacny-system-dodavatelov/).
**(O) Not named in Addendum A — this is an addition.** It is a distinct route from the food DNS and worth registering in alongside it, because it covers defence procurement more broadly than food.
**Verify:** the current qualification categories and whether food/subsistence is an eligible category.

### 2.4 Food business registration — the actual day-one compliance
**(F/R)** Before any of the above produces revenue you need: the s.r.o., the food trade licence (*živnosť* for food production/trade), and registration of the food business with the district veterinary and food authority (**RVPS**, under ŠVPS SR). For a kitting/packing operation handling only pre-packed shelf-stable goods, this is a registration, not an approval — materially lighter than a production plant. **Verify with RVPS Rimavská Sobota directly.**
See `03-certification-route.md` — this, plus HACCP, is your entire day-one quality requirement. Nothing more.

## 3. NATO registrations

### 3.1 NCAGE — do this first, it is free
**What it is (F):** the NATO Commercial and Government Entity code — your unique identifier across NATO systems.
**How (F):**
1. Go to the **NSPA CageTool**: [eportal.nspa.nato.int/Codification/CageTool/home](https://eportal.nspa.nato.int/Codification/CageTool/home)
2. Enter the organisation name, click **Search** to check the existing NCAGE database.
3. If not found, click **Request New**.
4. In the *"1. Start: Country check"* form, select entity type and processing timeframe — **Routine: 10 calendar days** is the recommended option.
5. NSPA emails a validation link to the contact address given. **You must click it** or the request dies.
6. NSPA forwards the request to the Slovak national authority for processing and assignment; the code typically appears in the central database within **~3 business days** of that.

**Slovak authority (F):** ÚOŠKŠOK (Úrad pre obrannú štandardizáciu, kodifikáciu a štátne overovanie kvality), Trenčín — it assigns NCAGE codes to Slovakia-based suppliers and maintains the data in the MCC WEB codification system, which feeds NATO's NMCRL database at NSPA.
**Contact (F):** `codify@mil.sk` · application page: [uosksok.sk — Žiadosť na NCAGE](https://www.uosksok.sk/?page=X174_SK)
**Cost (F):** free. **Prerequisite:** an existing legal entity (IČO).

### 3.2 NSPA Source File — the thing that actually makes you visible
**What it is (F):** a centralised database of NSPA's past, present and potential vendors, including performance and capabilities. The data in it supports source selection *and* the determination of whether a company is eligible for a contract award.
**Why it is not optional (F):** Source File registration is **necessary to be invited to a solicitation** and for a bid to be evaluated and considered for award. Without it, you are invisible.
**Eligibility criteria (F):** legal residency · national eligibility status · present capability · financial standing · past performance. NSPA procurement is **normally limited to firms located in NATO member nations** — a Slovak s.r.o. qualifies on residency.
**What you get (F):** on registration, a vendor account is created in **NSPA eProcurement**, giving access to **eBid** (electronic bidding for supply), access to restricted solicitations, and electronic bid submission. You then log in to maintain the company profile and users.
**Where:** [nspa.nato.int — Vendor Registration](https://www.nspa.nato.int/business/procurement/vendor) · portal: [eportal.nspa.nato.int](https://eportal.nspa.nato.int/)
**Cost:** free. **Sequence:** NCAGE first, then Source File.

**(R) Honest expectation-setting.** "Present capability, financial standing and past performance" are real screens. A newly-formed s.r.o. with no trading history will register successfully but will not be competitive on NSPA solicitations for 2–3 years. **Register anyway** — it is free, it is a prerequisite that takes weeks to clear, and having it done removes a blocker at the moment an opportunity appears. Treat it as optionality, not pipeline.

### 3.3 MO SR NSIP database
**What it is (F):** participation in international tenders and involvement in **NATO Security Investment Programme (NSIP)** projects is only possible if a company is registered in the MO SR NSIP database. Registration requests are submitted in writing; requests for competency declarations should be submitted well in advance, via the designated email contacts. Companies may be asked to submit references or full documentation confirming compliance with registration and tender participation conditions.
**(O) Relevance to you:** low for food, real for **Tier 4 deployable modules** (containerised field kitchens/bakeries/packing units). NSIP funds infrastructure and installations. Park this until Tier 4 is live — but know it exists, because it is a separate gate that takes time.

### 3.4 AQAP 2110 — the expensive trap
**What it is (F):** the NATO quality management standard (Allied Quality Assurance Publication) covering quality assurance in design, development and production of defence products or services. It is **built on ISO 9001 and extends it** with defence-specific elements. It is intended mainly for bilateral contracts supplying the Ministry of Defence, or project management contracts. In Slovakia, **ÚOŠKŠOK performs the audits and other tasks of state quality verification** (*štátne overovanie kvality*).
**The claim you will hear (F, from certification vendors):** "without AQAP 2110 it is difficult to obtain large NATO contracts, and it is practically a necessary condition for participation in defence tenders."
**(O) Why that claim should not drive your decision yet:**
- AQAP 2110 **requires ISO 9001 as its base** (F). You do not have ISO 9001 and should not buy it in 2026–27 (see `03-certification-route.md`).
- It is aimed at **design, development and production** of defence materiel. A Tier 0 broker reselling certified rations back-to-back is not doing design or development.
- Cost **(R): €10–25k+** all-in (consultancy + system build + audit), plus 9–18 months of management attention.
- Addendum A's own quality ladder puts it last and conditional: *"AQAP (NATO quality) only when a defence buyer asks and pays for it."* That is the correct rule.

**(O) Decision: defer. Revisit only when a named buyer names it in a live tender you can otherwise win.** Then negotiate it into the contract price.

**Slovak AQAP 2110 providers, for when the time comes (F):** [IAA SK](https://iaa.sk/sqap-2110/) · [CEMS](https://www.cems.sk/skolenie/281-poziadavky-standardu-aqap-2110-na-overovanie-kvality-v-navrhu-vyvoji-a-vyrobe) · [ECTA](https://www.ecta.sk/en/services/standard-aqap-2110/) · [TSU](https://www.iso.sk/novinky/43-aqap-2110-certifikacia). Get three quotes when you get there; do not accept the first.
