## 1. Premises — a correction to my earlier advice

**You are right and `02`/`08` were wrong for the early phase.** Those files recommended a yard plus food-grade container conversion. The logic was resale value and grant site-lock, and that logic still holds at Tier 1 scale. But for getting started it is beaten outright by an existing approved kitchen, because **a container conversion costs €20,000–40,000 (R) to create exactly the things an approved kitchen already has:**

| What makes premises "already hygienic" | Cost to create from bare shell **(R)** |
|---|---|
| Coved, impervious, drained flooring | €4,000–12,000 |
| Washable wall surfaces to height | €2,000–6,000 |
| Hand-wash stations, hot water, elbow/foot taps | €1,500–4,000 |
| Floor drainage + grease trap | €2,000–8,000 |
| Mechanical ventilation / extraction | €3,000–10,000 |
| Separate clean/unclean flows, changing area, WC | €2,000–6,000 |
| Pest-proofing, shatterproof lighting | €1,000–3,000 |
| **Total you avoid by renting approved space** | **€15,500–49,000** |

**(F) The regulatory requirement this reflects:** raw-material reception and storage must be separated from preparation and production areas, with clear separation between clean and unclean parts of the operation.

> **(O) So the correct Phase 0–1 answer is: rent approved space, own no building, convert nothing.** Revisit containers at Tier 1 only if a grant's site-lock clause or a deployable-module (Tier 4) opportunity makes them worth it.

### One nuance to verify before signing anything
**(R) Registration does not transfer with the building.** An approved kitchen's compliance history belongs to the previous operator. **You** must register your own food business with RVPS for that address. What you inherit is *compliant construction* — which makes your registration straightforward rather than a building project. **Confirm this with RVPS Rimavská Sobota before you commit to a lease** (`03` §6).

**(R) Indicative cost for ~150 m² of approved kitchen: €375–750/month rent plus €150–400/month utilities.** That is the number in the budget.

**(F) Honest search result: no specific HACCP-kitchen listing in Rimavská Sobota surfaced.** Nationally there are ~94 restaurant rental listings; the RS district shows 352 property listings and ~21 non-residential rentals, but none identifiably an approved kitchen. **(O) This is a phone-and-walk market, not a portal market.** Expect to find it by asking, not by browsing.

**Sources:** [Nehnuteľnosti.sk — RS non-residential rentals](https://www.nehnutelnosti.sk/rimavska-sobota/nebytove-priestory/prenajom/) · [Zoznamrealit — RS warehouses & halls](https://www.zoznamrealit.sk/prenajom/nebytove-priestory/sklady-a-haly/rimavska-sobota) · [Bazoš reality — RS halls](https://reality.bazos.sk/inzerat/196049704/sklady-alebo-vyrobne-haly-na-prenajom-v-rimavskej-sobote.php) · [Nehnuteľnosti.sk — restaurant rentals](https://www.nehnutelnosti.sk/restauracie/prenajom/) · [Azet — RS gastronomy & catering directory](https://www.azet.sk/katalog/gastronomia-a-catering/abc/rimavska-sobota/)

### Search protocol — do this in one afternoon
1. **Call RVPS Rimavská Sobota.** Ask which premises in the district hold or recently held food-business registration. **(O) They know the entire approved estate in the district. This is the single most efficient call available to you**, and nobody thinks to make it.
2. **Call the municipality (mesto Rimavská Sobota) and 3–4 village councils**, Ožďany included: do any school or works canteens have idle capacity to rent?
3. **Call the catering firms** in the Azet directory above, starting with IK Catering.
4. **Ask the district office / ÚPSVaR** about closed food businesses — they see closures first.
5. Only then browse the portals.

### Due diligence when you view
Flooring coved and sound · walls washable to height · hot water and hand-wash stations present and working · **extraction intact** (most commonly stripped) · floor drainage and grease trap · separate clean/unclean routes · changing area and WC · **dry storage you can lock and vermin-proof** · three-phase power · **the previous RVPS registration number and why it closed** · lease term **12 months with a break clause** and no fit-out obligation (`02` §3).

## 4. Second-hand versus new

**(O) Buy used for anything mechanical, new for anything that must be calibrated or certified.**

| Buy **used** | Buy **new** |
|---|---|
| Flow-wrapper, shrink tunnel, conveyors, benches, pallet wrapper, vacuum sealer, racking | **Calibrated scales** (legal metrology), **label printer** (support + consumables), nest trays (bespoke anyway), camera, PPE and anything food-contact |

**(F/O) Used-equipment protocol** (`08` §3): never buy unseen · demand a video or witnessed run with *your* product · insist on CE marking and a Declaration of Conformity · prefer post-2012 control systems for spares · **budget 10–15% of purchase price for commissioning, spares and format parts** — the line everyone forgets.

**Channels (F):** [Machineseeker](https://www.machineseeker.com/Packaging-machinery/ci-13) (1,834+ used listings) · [Bazoš.sk](https://stroje.bazos.sk/inzeraty/baliac%C3%AD-stroj/) · [Kabek/CzechTrade](https://kabek.czechtrade.sk/pouzite-baliace-stroje) (used horizontal wrappers) · [GPR.sk](https://gpr.sk/sk/vakuove-baliace-stroje/) · [Pekastroj](https://www.pekastroj.sk/48-baliace-stroje-balicky).
