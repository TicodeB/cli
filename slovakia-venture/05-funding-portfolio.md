## 2. The channel Addendum A under-weighted: Just Transition Fund

**(F) Rimavská Sobota is an eligible district under the Fond na spravodlivú transformáciu (Just Transition Fund).** In the Banská Bystrica region the eligible districts are **Brezno, Revúca, Rimavská Sobota, Zvolen, Žiar nad Hronom, Žarnovica and Banská Štiavnica**. Total allocation across the three eligible Slovak regions (Trenčín, Košice, Banská Bystrica) is **€459m**.

**(F)** The fund supports creation of new jobs, retraining, **support for SMEs including productive investments**, development of micro-enterprises and start-ups — "especially in new, emerging and transformed sectors of the economy" — plus science/research/innovation and circular economy. One call allows micro, small and medium enterprises to apply for grants **up to €800,000**.

**(O) Why this matters more than its position in Addendum A's ladder suggests.** JTF is *designed* for exactly your profile: a new manufacturing SME creating jobs in a transitioning district, in an emerging sector. The "new, emerging and transformed sectors" language is a gift — European civil preparedness and defence-adjacent food security is precisely a new sector, and you can document EU policy driving it (EU Stockpiling Strategy COM(2025) 528, Preparedness Union Strategy). **(R) I would rate a well-built JTF application at or above the regional contribution on expected value**, because the ticket size is an order of magnitude larger.

**Action:** ask the MIRRI regional centre in Rimavská Sobota, in the same conversation as the regional contribution question, **which JTF calls are currently open or scheduled for Banská Bystrica region and what the next submission window is.** One phone call covers both.

## 4. Regional investment aid — why it is not for you yet

**(F)** The framework is **Act 57/2018** on regional investment aid, with **Government Regulation 195/2018** setting conditions, maximum aid intensity and maximum aid amounts by region. Aid requires the investment project to have **significant regional contribution**, and conditions include a **minimum investment value and a minimum number of new jobs created**. Maximum intensity: **25%** of eligible costs for western Slovak districts, **35%** for all other districts — which includes Rimavská Sobota. Regulation **48/2026** took effect **01/04/2026**. Applications for **mimoriadna investičná pomoc** (extraordinary investment aid) may be submitted from **01/04/2026**.

**(R) Why to park it:** the minimum investment threshold for industrial production is the binding constraint, and it is far above a €120–180k container line. This instrument is built for €1m+ projects. It becomes relevant only at **Tier 3**, and then it is worth 35% — genuinely large.

**(O) Plan for it anyway, in one specific way:** the 35% intensity applies to *eligible costs*, and the jobs condition is easier to satisfy in a district with Slovakia's highest unemployment. When Tier 3 is justified by contracts, this is the instrument that funds it. Do not contort the Tier 1 project to reach its threshold — that is how a €160k plan becomes a €600k plan for grant reasons, which is exactly the trap Addendum A's premortem describes.

**Sources:** [MHSR — investment aid legislation](https://www.economy.gov.sk/podpora-investicii/investicna-pomoc/legislativa) · [Regulation 195/2018 (Slov-Lex)](https://www.slov-lex.sk/ezbierky/pravne-predpisy/SK/ZZ/2018/195/) · [MHSR — 2026 regional contribution assessment](https://www.mhsr.sk/uploads/files/gGpsGzHR.pdf) · [MHSR — mimoriadna investičná pomoc](https://www.economy.gov.sk/podpora-investicii/podpora-investicii/investicna-pomoc/mimoriadna-investicna-pomoc)

## 5. Program Slovensko 2021–2027

**(F)** Relevant strands: support for **productive investments in SMEs in the regions** through acquisition of long-term tangible and intangible assets; **STEP platform** support for innovation via long-term tangible assets producing innovated products/processes; and MH SR delivering investment support **preferentially as returnable support — guaranteed loans with a grant component**. The **Just Transition Fund** strand specifically targets SME development including productive investments and micro-enterprises/start-ups.

**(O) Read that "returnable support with a grant component" carefully — it is the single most useful sentence in Slovak SME funding.** It means the realistic offer is a *guaranteed loan where part of the principal is forgiven*, not a pure grant. Addendum A's line 11 ("SIH / MH SR NDF III, 0–25% principal forgiven") is the same mechanism. Build the Tier 1 funding stack expecting a guaranteed loan with partial forgiveness, and treat pure grants as upside.

## 7. The grant rule that kills more projects than markets do

**(F/O)** Carried from Addendum A because it is the most expensive rule to learn late: **no binding orders before grant approval.** Most Slovak and EU instruments treat a project as ineligible if work started, or equipment was ordered, before the application or the decision. Related traps:
- **Site lock-in / durability period.** Grants typically require the funded asset to stay at the funded location for 3–5 years. **(O) The container line design in `08` is partly a defence against this** — but if a grant funds it, the grant's site condition still binds. Read the durability clause before signing.
- **Own-contribution proof.** You will be asked to evidence the co-financing share before the decision. Have it liquid and documented.
- **Double funding.** The same cost cannot be funded twice. Map each cost line to exactly one instrument in the stack.
