# 02 — Premises: Rimavská Sobota

**Conclusion first (O): do not acquire premises in 2026.** Tier 0 needs a registered seat and nothing else. Tier 1 needs a yard with utilities, not a hall. The single most expensive error available here is signing a hall lease or purchase before revenue — it is item #1 in Addendum A's premortem ("the hall was rented from month one and burned €22k of dead rent").

## 1. Premises requirement by tier

| Tier | Activity | Space needed | Form | Cost **(R)** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **0** | Broking, back-to-back supply, no custody | **None** | Registered seat in RS district (virtual office / accountant's address) | **€10–40/month** |
| **1** | Kitting, assembly, re-packing | 150–400 m² usable **+** hardstanding for containers | **Rented yard or small unit**, containers owned | €3–8/m²/month **(R)** |
| **2** | Reserve holding & rotation | 500–1,500 m² dry warehouse, racked | Rented warehouse, ideally within the customer's required response radius | €2.5–5/m²/month **(R)** |
| **3** | Manufacturing (bake / retort / lyo) | 1,000–2,000 m² + utilities + effluent | Purpose-built or heavy-refit hall | €450–700k capex **(F, Addendum A)** |

> **(O) The seat matters legally, the yard matters operationally, and they need not be the same place.** Put the registered seat in the Rimavská Sobota district on day one — it is the eligibility condition for the regional contribution and the district's aid instruments. That costs tens of euros a month and can be done before you know where you will actually operate.

## 2. What is actually in Rimavská Sobota

### 2.1 MH Invest strategic industrial park — the state park
**(F)** A state-prepared industrial park at Rimavská Sobota, developed by the state company **MH Invest**:
- Total land take **169.81 ha**; the strategic park itself **65.08 ha**.
- Land for future investors divided into **four separate units / project stages**.
- Infrastructure built; the park "has been lit" (utilities and lighting delivered) and MH Invest has confirmed all key milestones met.
- **Anchor investor: Winkelmann Building + Industry (WBI) / reflexGroup**, whose plant makes metal hot-water tanks for heat pumps and water heaters — already in operation.
- **Expansion underway:** initial earthworks and archaeological survey started on ~**23 ha** in the expansion area at *Kľačianske lúky*.
- Marketed as offering modern production and warehouse halls adaptable to tenant needs, including **build-to-suit (BTS)** units.
- **(F)** Land prices were **not disclosed** in available sources.

**(O) Assessment: not your building, but absolutely your phone call.** A 65 ha state strategic park with a German anchor tenant is structured for €10m+ investors, and a €160k kitting operation is not its target. But two things make it worth one conversation:
1. **BTS and adaptable units** imply a landlord willing to discuss smaller lettings, especially in early-stage parks that want occupancy.
2. **Proximity to an anchor manufacturer creates demand you can serve** — a new plant with hundreds of staff needs canteen supply, and it sits in your district.

**Action (R):** email MH Invest asking (a) whether any sub-1,000 m² units or yard space are available or planned, (b) indicative land/lease pricing, (c) power and gas capacity available at the plots. Cost: one email. Note `www.mhinvest.sk` was unreachable from the build environment — use a browser.

### 2.3 Open-market stock — two concrete comparables
**(F)** From current listings in the district:
- **Industrial compound**, flat site **14,741 m²**: three production halls of **517, 816 and 516 m²**, administrative building **395 m²**, **transformer station 171 m²**, plus garages.
- **Business compound** on **2,210 m²**: option to buy the hall alone — **427.6 m²** with surrounding land of **1,191 m²** — at **€178,000 excl. VAT**.

**(O) Use the second listing as a calibration, not a purchase.** €178,000 for 427 m² is approximately the **entire Tier 1 capex budget** (€120–180k). Spending it on a building buys you a roof and no revenue capability; spending it on the kitting line buys a revenue capability that can move and can be resold. This comparable is the clearest possible argument for the container strategy.

**(R) But note the first listing carefully.** An existing compound with **three halls and its own transformer station** is the single most interesting Tier 2/Tier 3 object in the district, because a transformer station and hardstanding are the expensive parts to create from scratch. Get the asking price and the available power capacity. If the price is sane, it is a candidate for **Tier 2 warehousing plus a future Tier 3 hall on one site** — post-Gate 1, not now.

## 3. Tier 1 site specification — what to actually look for

Requirements for a container-based kitting operation. **(R)** throughout; confirm against the final line layout in `08`.

| Requirement | Spec | Why it is on this list |
|---|---|---|
| Hardstanding | ≥300 m², load-bearing, drained | Containers need level, stable ground; mud fails a food audit |
| Electrical supply | **3-phase, ≥40 kVA** reserved capacity | Flow-wrapper + shrink tunnel + HVAC. Undersized supply is the classic retrofit cost |
| Water + foul drainage | Potable, plus WC/wash facilities | Non-negotiable food-hygiene requirement |
| Vehicle access | Rigid truck / 40ft delivery, turning circle | You receive on pallets and dispatch on pallets |
| Dry storage | 100–200 m² weatherproof, vermin-proofed | Component buffer stock |
| Security | Fenced, gated, alarmed, CCTV | **(F)** Addendum A flags real sabotage risk in this sector after the thwarted arson plot against a Slovak drone plant (08/2026) |
| Lease flexibility | **12 months, break clause, no fit-out obligation** | The point of containers is optionality; do not sign it away in the lease |
| Grant compatibility | Landlord consent to the site being named in a grant | Grants impose a 3–5 year durability period at the funded site |

### Electrical connection — the long-lead item
**(F)** Rimavská Sobota is in the **Stredoslovenská distribučná (SSD)** area. The distribution operator has **30 calendar days** to issue a written statement on a connection request; if the request is incomplete it must notify deficiencies immediately and require correction within **15 days**. Where technical conditions do not permit the full requested capacity, the maximum reserved capacity is agreed at a lower value set by the technical connection conditions.

**(O) Practical implication:** if the site's existing reserved capacity is insufficient, you are looking at a 30-day statement plus works — easily 3–6 months **(R)**. **Therefore: make existing adequate reserved capacity a selection criterion, not a problem to solve later.** Ask every prospective landlord for the current *maximum reserved capacity* in kW before viewing. This one question eliminates most unsuitable sites for free.
**Sources:** [SSD — forms for non-household customers](https://www.ssd.sk/buxus/docs/dokumenty/spolocne_dokumenty_pre_vsetky_segmenty/N3%C5%BDiados%C5%A5%20o%20pripojenie%20POD_reader.pdf) · [SSD — applications and forms](https://www.ssd.sk/vyrobcovia-elektriny/ziadosti-a-formulare?page_id=4328)

## 4. Scoring matrix

Weighted for a **Tier 1** decision. Score each candidate 1–5, multiply, total. **(O)** Weights are mine; adjust them, but adjust them *before* you see the candidates, not after.

| Criterion | Weight | Why weighted this way |
|---|---|---|
| Existing reserved electrical capacity | **20%** | Longest lead time, highest retrofit cost |
| Lease flexibility (term, break, exit) | **20%** | Optionality is the whole strategy |
| Total monthly cost (rent + service + energy) | 15% | Dead rent is the named premortem killer |
| Condition of hardstanding + truck access | 15% | Cheap to check, expensive to fix |
| Dry storage included | 10% | Otherwise a second lease |
| Security / fencing | 10% | Sector-specific risk |
| Distance from RS district seat & co-packers | 5% | Matters less than people think at this volume |
| Landlord's grant cooperation | 5% | Binary blocker if refused |

**Decision rule (O):** do not view any site scoring below 3 on *either* electrical capacity *or* lease flexibility, regardless of total score. Those two are gating, not tradeable.

## 5. Recommendation

1. **Now:** registered seat in RS district. €10–40/month. Nothing more.
2. **Q4 2026:** three enquiries — MH Invest, PPG Gemer, and the 14,741 m² compound agent. Ask only about unit size, indicative price, and **reserved kW**. No viewings yet.
3. **Post-Gate 1 (01/2028), and only then:** shortlist and score yards. Target a 12-month term with a break clause.
4. **Post-Gate 2:** revisit the multi-hall compound with the transformer station as a combined Tier 2 + Tier 3 site.

**(O) The discipline to state plainly:** every month you do not hold premises is a month of preserved optionality and zero dead rent. In a venture whose named failure mode is fixed cost arriving before revenue, "no premises yet" is a strategy, not a delay.
